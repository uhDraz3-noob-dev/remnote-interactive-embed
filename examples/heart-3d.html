SPATIAL EXPLORER

A heart with depth

Turn the model, select a chamber, and reveal the cavities.

Start with 2D, or load the plugin’s bundled 3D engine.

Original stylized teaching model, not exact anatomy or a physiological simulator. The filling control changes cavity size; it does not model pressures, valves, or a cardiac cycle. Blue/red identify right/left chambers. Drag to orbit in 3D; camera buttons provide an alternative.
